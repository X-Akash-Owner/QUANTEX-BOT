### Paying with Auto Payment (instant)

1. Tap a plan. You see the final price per month.
2. Choose a method:

| Button | Network |
|---|---|
| **BINANCE PAY** | Binance Pay ID `1133439955` |
| **USDT — TRC20** | USDT on TRON |
| **USDT — BEP20** | USDT on BNB Smart Chain |

3. The bot creates an **order** with the exact USDT amount, the address / Pay ID and an expiry time. Send exactly that amount.
4. Tap **VERIFY PAYMENT** and paste your **Binance Pay Order ID**, the **blockchain transaction hash**, or the **Binance off-chain transfer ID**.
5. When verified, your plan is activated automatically.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **PAYMENT STATUS** | Shows status, order ID, package, amount, method and expiry |
| **VERIFY AGAIN** | Try verifying again (e.g. if the transaction was still confirming) |
| **CONTACT OWNER** | Get help from the owner |
| **Back to Plans** | Back |

*"This payment order has expired."* means you must create a new order.

> You get a reminder before your plan expires, with **Renew Now**.
