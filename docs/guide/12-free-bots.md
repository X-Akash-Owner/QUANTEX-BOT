## How to use a free bot

1. Tap a green **NUMBER 0x** button. A page describes the bot and its features.
2. Tap the **OPEN …** button on that page. The bot's app opens directly inside Telegram.
3. Use it. Tap **Back** to return to the Free Bots list.
