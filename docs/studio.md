## ✨ What's in v1.0

### 🧙 9-step bot wizard
**Token → Brand → Support → Timezone → Welcome → About → Help → Features → Force Join → Review → DEPLOY.**
- The bot token is checked privately with a live animation, and your token message is deleted.
- Every step has **Back**. The review screen has **DEPLOY**, **Back** and **CANCEL**.
- **Animated deploy**: a ~4-second progress bar runs through 6 stages, then **YOUR BOT IS LIVE** shows your bot's link.

### 🏷 Your brand everywhere
- Your brand name replaces **QUANTEX** in menus, welcome text, signals, results, chart titles, watermarks and partial reports, **in the same font**.
- Your own **support account** on every Support / Contact button.
- Your own **timezone** for every time your bot shows.

### 🎨 Templates & design
- **5 Welcome · 5 About · 5 Help** premium templates with preview → confirm.
- **3 chart templates**: CLASSIC QUANTUM · AI TERMINAL · PULSE PRO, with a live preview and your own watermark.
- **3 Live Signal templates** (CLASSIC · BOX FRAME · PREMIUM STAR) and **3 Live Result templates** (CLASSIC · CROWN SURESHOT · PREMIUM STAR). One choice covers Live Signal and Live Session.
- **Future Signal, AXTIRON FS and News Signal** template sections (open to every package).
- **Buttons**: rename, recolour (Blue / Green / Red), **BOLD / MONOSPACE** font, **premium emoji icons**, reset.
- **Drag-and-drop home layout** in the web app: move buttons between rows with a 3D preview, then Save / Reset.

### 🧩 Features & brokers
- Up to **23 QUANTEX features** as buttons in your bot: Live Session, Schedule, 4 Checkers, 5 Future Signal modes + **AXTIRON FS** (PRO / ELITE), Live Signal, Live Payouts, News Signal, 4 Tools, Profile, About, Reviews, Help.
- **Brokers**: QUOTEX, TRADOWIX and BINOLLA, depending on the package.
- **Force Join**: users must join your channel (public or private) first.

### 🧠 Strategy Studio *(per bot, fully isolated)*
- **MAIN BOT** analyzers: QUANTUM SCAN · SMART FOCUS · HYBRID ENGINE · Hybrid Engine V2 Astra · QUANTEX TURBO.
- **10 pre-made** strategies, **7 trading styles**, a **custom 5-indicator mix**.
- **20-indicator BUILDER** with editable values and an "at least N must agree" rule.
- **ELITE: two strategies at once**, each a named button with its own description.
- Each bot's strategy and market cooldowns belong to that bot only.

### 🌐 STUDIO CONTROL web app
Tabs: **Overview · Brand · Features · Strategy · Texts · Buttons**. Every change is saved at once and the panel refreshes instantly.

### 🛠 Your own admin panel
- `/admin` inside your bot: **Users, Licenses, Broadcast, Banned, Stats, Logs, Access (Open / Licensed), Admin Web, Guide**.
- Per user: **7 / 30 days / permanent licence, remove, ban / unban, reset usage**.
- Commands: `/stats /users /broadcast /ban /unban /banlist /addlicense /removelicense /checklicense /licensedusers /access`.
- **Admin Web**: Overview, Users, Limits (daily Live / Future per tier), Logs, Bot Control.

### 📊 Management
- **MY BOTS**: Start / Stop, Customize, Change Token, Analytics, Delete.
- **ANALYTICS**: users, active today, actions, signals per bot.
- **SUBSCRIBE**: monthly or yearly, Binance Pay / USDT TRC20 / USDT BEP20, instant activation, renewals add to your current expiry.
- **Reminders**: one evening reminder 3 days before a package ends, with **RENEW**.

### 🔐 Privacy & isolation
- Each bot's users, licences, bans, limits, texts, strategy and logs are **separate**: nothing mixes with the main bot or your other bots.
- Main-bot plans and admin rights **never** carry into a Studio bot.
- Bot tokens are never shown or written to logs.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Create a bot with **@BotFather** (`/newbot`) and copy the token.
2. Open [@QuantexBinaryTools_bot](https://t.me/QuantexBinaryTools_bot) → **Q-BOT STUDIO** → **CREATE**.
3. Paste the token, then follow the 9 steps.
4. Tap **DEPLOY**, open your new bot and send `/start`.
5. Fine-tune everything later from **MYBOTS → CUSTOMIZE** or **STUDIO CONTROL**, and manage users with `/admin` inside your bot.
